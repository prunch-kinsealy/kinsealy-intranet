CDM Pre-Appointment Form
Please complete every section below as fully as you can, then return this form to reception before your Chronic Disease Management (CDM) or Prevention Programme appointment. If a family member, next of kin, or neighbour is helping you complete this, that's absolutely fine.
🍽️ Fasting required. Please fast from midnight the night before your appointment — no food or drink except plain water. Continue all regular medications as normal with a small sip of water. Fasting is needed for accurate blood results (glucose, cholesterol, triglycerides).

Before you begin, if possible have to hand:
  • Weight scales — to enter your weight in Section 4
  • A measuring tape — to measure your waist in Section 4
  • A home blood pressure machine, if you have one — take a few readings over several mornings and note your average in the relevant condition section below. Home readings are often more accurate than surgery readings ("white coat hypertension"). If you are declining the 24-hour BP monitor (ABPM) in Section 7, a home BP reading is required.

7. 24-Hour Blood Pressure Monitor (ABPM) Consent
The ABPM is a small device worn on your upper arm for 24 hours. It automatically checks your blood pressure every 30 minutes during the day and every hour at night, giving a more accurate picture than a single surgery reading. It's recommended for patients aged 50 or over with hypertension, cardiovascular disease, type 2 diabetes, heart failure, atrial fibrillation, or a history of stroke/TIA. If unsure, your nurse will advise you at your appointment.
Yes, I consent to wearing the ABPM if my nurse recommends it
I'd like to discuss this further at my appointment
No, I decline the ABPM at this time
If you consent to ABPM, please note:
  • The monitor is practice equipment loaned to you for 24 hours — please keep it dry, safe, and in working condition.
  • Please do not tamper with or attempt to repair the device.
  • You must return the device to the practice on the following working day.
  • Failure to return the device, or damage due to negligence, may result in a charge for repair or replacement.
If you are declining the ABPM, please provide a home blood pressure reading (average of several mornings) in the relevant condition section above, where available.
